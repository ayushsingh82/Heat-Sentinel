# FortyGuard Hackathon'26 — Rules

Source: https://www.fortyguard.com/hackathon26

- Global, virtual, free to enter.
- Teams: solo or up to 3 members. No climate background required — open to AI engineers, ML researchers, developers, students, designers, climate-tech builders, urban/energy specialists, geospatial professionals.
- Build using FortyGuard's Temperature API (hyperlocal urban temperature intelligence, measured 2m above ground, 10 mi² resolution, NVIDIA-recognized).
- Hardware prizes (NVIDIA Jetson kits) subject to availability and eligibility requirements — terms and conditions apply.
- All participants receive a certificate of completion; top teams get career opportunities and partner visibility.
