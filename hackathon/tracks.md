# FortyGuard Hackathon'26 — Challenge Tracks

Source: https://www.fortyguard.com/hackathon26

Seven tracks. Pick one or combine. Every track builds on the **Temperature API®**.

---

## Track 01 — Resilient Cities & Infrastructure

Design cooler, smarter cities using hyperlocal temperature intelligence. Build AI
systems that help urban planners, residents, and emergency services navigate heat
at a city scale.

- **Build examples:** Cool Route Planner · Public Asset Heat Audit · Digital Twin Simulation
- **Technologies:** Temperature API® · GIS · Urban AI · Climate Intelligence

---

## Track 02 — Future Buildings & Energy

Optimize buildings, cooling systems, and energy use with AI-driven heat insights.
Reduce consumption and improve efficiency for homes, offices, and commercial
infrastructure.

- **Build examples:** HVAC Optimization · Energy Forecasting · Retrofit ROI
- **Technologies:** Temperature API® · ML Models · Energy Systems · IoT

> This repo's **Building Heat-Risk Copilot** targets this track (plus Agentic AI).

---

## Track 03 — Industrial & Enterprise

Turn heat intelligence into operational and business decisions. Help enterprises
manage logistics, data center performance, and industrial risk through AI-powered
thermal analysis.

- **Build examples:** Logistics Heat Risk · Data Center Cooling · Worker Safety Dashboard
- **Technologies:** Temperature API® · AI · Risk Analysis · Operations

---

## Track 04 — Government & Environment

Support public safety, resilience, and environmental planning with AI. Build tools
for policymakers and city agencies to act on real-time and predictive heat
intelligence.

- **Build examples:** Heat Vulnerability Map · Agricultural Stress Monitor · Climate Resilience Planner
- **Technologies:** Temperature API® · Policy AI · GIS · Open Data

---

## Track 05 — Model Designing

Build AI and machine learning models that transform raw temperature data into
actionable insights. Create forecasting engines, anomaly detectors, or decision
models for heat intelligence.

- **Build examples:** Heat Forecasting Model · Anomaly Detector · Risk Classifier
- **Technologies:** Temperature API® · Machine Learning · Forecasting · Python

---

## Track 06 — Agentic AI

Create autonomous AI agents that use FortyGuard APIs to analyze, decide, and
automate heat-related workflows without human intervention. Push the frontier of
AI-native applications.

- **Build examples:** Heat Response Agent · API Orchestration Bot · Alert Automation Engine
- **Technologies:** Temperature API® · AI Agents · LLMs · Workflow Automation

> Secondary track for this repo.

---

## Track 07 — Data Analysis & Correlation

Discover how temperature influences people, infrastructure, and business outcomes
through rigorous data analysis. Surface insights that inform better policy,
product, and planning decisions.

- **Build examples:** Heat Equity Analysis · Economic Impact Study · Productivity Correlation
- **Technologies:** Temperature API® · Data Science · Statistics · Visualization
